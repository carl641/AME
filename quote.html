Before you request a quote.

Is the quote free?

Yes. There is no charge for a quote or for the design review that comes with it, and no obligation to place the order.

What do you need to give me a price?

A 3D model (STEP preferred; Parasolid, IGES and STL also work), the material, the quantity and your target date. A drawing or a note on critical tolerances, surface finish, heat treatment and testing makes the quote more accurate.

What drives the price of a metal 3D printed part?

Build height, part volume, support structures, alloy and post processing. Small simple parts start in the low hundreds of dollars; large or complex Inconel and copper alloy builds run into the thousands. The cost guide walks through each driver.

Can you sign an NDA before I send files?

Yes. Most of our work is under NDA and we are used to program specific handling requirements. Mention it in the form or call the shop and we will sign first.

What quantities do you take on?

One prototype through pilot series production. A single plate on the M 400 can carry one large component or dozens of small ones, and repeat runs use the same locked parameters.

Do you work with companies outside Alabama?

Yes. We ship nationwide and most of our customers are outside the state. Local teams near Redstone Arsenal and Marshall can visit the shop in Huntsville and pick up parts.

What if my part is not a fit for laser powder bed fusion?

We will tell you. If another process or another alloy is the better answer, you will hear that from us rather than a quote for a part that should not be printed.
